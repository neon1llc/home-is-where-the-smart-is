# Changelog

Home Is Where the Smart Is releases use `v0.MINOR.PATCH` until the first complete release, v1.0.0, following neon1's [versioning standard](https://neon1.tech/knowledge/standards/versioning/). Each version below has an annotated tag on the commit it names; the release workflow adds missing tags and never moves one.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-12

### Added
- Twelve handbook chapters: the household map, accounts and passwords, backups, the router and Wi-Fi, devices and updates, kids' devices, helping aging parents, the smart home, money and identity, buying and retiring devices, emergencies and digital legacy, and when something goes wrong.
- Templates: a household tech sheet, a device inventory, an emergency card, and an account recovery sheet.
- Checkups: yearly, for a new device, and for visiting parents.
