---
title: New device checklist
description: "Thirty minutes when a phone, computer, or tablet arrives, so it starts out safe and backed up."
---

- [ ] **Updates first.** Install every available update before anything else.
- [ ] **Sign in** with the person's own account, inside the family group for children.
- [ ] **Screen lock** of six digits or more, with face or fingerprint unlock.
- [ ] **Encryption** on. It's automatic on phones. On computers, turn on FileVault or BitLocker and save the recovery key in the password manager.
- [ ] **Find my device** on.
- [ ] **Automatic updates** on for the system and apps.
- [ ] **Backups** on, and the first backup completed.
- [ ] **Password manager** installed and signed in.
- [ ] **Privacy settings** reviewed: location, ad tracking, and app permissions.
- [ ] **For children:** age-appropriate controls on ([chapter 6](../handbook/06-kids-devices.md)).
- [ ] **Added** to the [device inventory](../templates/device-inventory.csv), with the date updates end.
- [ ] **The old device** retired properly ([chapter 10](../handbook/10-buying-and-retiring.md)).
