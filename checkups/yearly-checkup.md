---
title: Yearly checkup
description: "One afternoon a year to keep the household's tech in shape: accounts, backups, the router, devices, money, and emergency plans."
---

Work through it with the [household tech sheet](../templates/household-tech-sheet.md) open, and update it as you go.

## Accounts

- [ ] Two-step sign-in still on for every person's email and bank.
- [ ] Recovery details checked on each [account recovery sheet](../templates/account-recovery-sheet.md).
- [ ] Password manager's breach and reuse report reviewed, and flagged passwords changed.
- [ ] People who've moved out removed from shared vaults and family groups.

## Backups

- [ ] Phone and computer backups ran recently, and cloud storage isn't full.
- [ ] A restore tested, with the date recorded.

## Router and network

- [ ] Router firmware current. If it no longer gets updates, plan a replacement.
- [ ] Devices on the network match the inventory; remove anything unknown.

## Devices

- [ ] Every device has updates, screen lock, encryption, and find my device on.
- [ ] Devices that stopped getting updates are retired ([chapter 10](../handbook/10-buying-and-retiring.md)).
- [ ] The [device inventory](../templates/device-inventory.csv) is up to date.

## Kids

- [ ] Controls adjusted for each child's age, and the family tech talk had again.

## Money and identity

- [ ] Credit reports checked at AnnualCreditReport.com for every adult.
- [ ] Credit freezes still in place, including for children.

## Emergencies

- [ ] Legacy contacts and password manager emergency access still correct.
- [ ] The [emergency card](../templates/emergency-card.md) current, and a second adult knows where it is.

## Parents

- [ ] Book the yearly [visiting parents](visiting-parents.md) checkup.
