# Household tech handbook

**A household tech handbook for the person who looks after a family's devices and accounts.** Passwords, backups, the router, kids' first phones, helping aging parents, and what to do when something breaks, written for the family's unofficial tech lead.

From [neon1](https://neon1.tech). *1 is glad to be of service.*

---

## Who it's for

Every household has one: the person everyone calls when the Wi-Fi drops, a parent's phone is full, or a strange text arrives. Home Is Where the Smart Is is for them. It assumes you're comfortable with technology, not that you're an expert, and it helps you set things up once so you're not fixing the same problems every month.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [`handbook/`](handbook/index.md) | Twelve chapters, from mapping your household's tech to planning for emergencies. Read them in order the first time, then use them as a reference. |
| [`templates/`](templates/index.md) | A household tech sheet, a device inventory, an emergency card, and an account recovery sheet. |
| [`checkups/`](checkups/index.md) | A yearly checkup, a new device checklist, and a checklist for visiting parents. |

## Start here

1. Read [chapter 1](handbook/01-household-map.md) and fill in the [household tech sheet](templates/household-tech-sheet.md). It takes an evening and makes everything else easier.
2. Do chapters [2 (accounts)](handbook/02-accounts-and-passwords.md) and [3 (backups)](handbook/03-backups.md) next. They prevent the two worst days: losing an email account and losing the photos.
3. Put the [yearly checkup](checkups/yearly-checkup.md) on the calendar.
