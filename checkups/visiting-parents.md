---
title: Visiting parents checklist
description: "About two hours, in person, to make a parent's devices simpler, safer, and easier for you to help with from far away."
---

Go at their pace, explain each change, and ask before you change anything. The goal is a setup they're comfortable running themselves.

## Their phone

- [ ] All updates installed; automatic updates on.
- [ ] Text size, bold text, and accessibility features they like.
- [ ] Your number in favorites; doctors and family saved as contacts.
- [ ] Unknown callers silenced or screened.
- [ ] Find My or Find My Device on, and location shared with you if they agree.
- [ ] Backups and photo sync on, with enough storage.

## Their accounts

- [ ] Two-step sign-in on email, using the method they find easiest.
- [ ] Recovery phone and email current; backup codes saved somewhere safe.
- [ ] A password manager or the phone's built-in one, set up together for their most-used accounts.
- [ ] Legacy and recovery contacts set ([chapter 11](../handbook/11-emergencies-and-legacy.md)).

## Their money

- [ ] Bank alerts for large withdrawals; a trusted contact named if the bank offers it.
- [ ] Credit freezes at all three bureaus.

## Their computer and home

- [ ] Updates, screen lock, and built-in antivirus on.
- [ ] Router admin password changed and firmware current, if they have their own internet.
- [ ] Remote screen sharing tested together.

## The conversation

- [ ] The pause: hang up, look up, call back.
- [ ] A family code word for emergency calls.
- [ ] "Call me first" for any request for money, gift cards, or remote access.
- [ ] Leave the [Phish Out of Water](https://github.com/neon1llc/phish-out-of-water) one-page takeaway by the phone.
